Privacy Policy

LeadLog CRM · Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GP Udvikling ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with the LeadLog CRM mobile application ("the App").

1. Data Controller

GP Udvikling
Contact: solgib@yahoo.com

2. Information We Collect

The App is a customer relationship management (CRM) tool for sales consultants. To provide its functionality, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form)
  • Business data you enter: leads (name, company, phone, email, status, source, notes), customers (name, company, contact person, phone, email, address), appointments (title, date/time, location, status), sales records (customer, date, type, amount, order number, notes), and sales-pitch notes
  • App preferences: your selected theme (light/dark) and language setting

The App does not require or request access to your device's camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core features (managing leads, customers, appointments, and sales)
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure and private to you
  • To let you export your own sales data (CSV/PDF) for your own records
  • To remember your display preferences (theme, language)

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your information to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

App data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and backend service that acts as our data processor. Access to your data is restricted to your own account through row-level security — no other user of the App can see your leads, customers, appointments, or sales records.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Your data may be processed by:

  • Supabase (database hosting and backend infrastructure), acting solely as a data processor on our behalf

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted as described in our Account Deletion instructions.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at solgib@yahoo.com.

8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by adult sales professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
